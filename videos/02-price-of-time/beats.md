# 02 — Your salary is the price of your time (visual beats)

Style: black background, dot grid, white line-art, bloom. One continuous canvas with no cuts, ease-in-out glides. Each label is uppercase and tracked.

| # | Voice | Visual | Label / number |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Your salary is the price of your time…" | The person (filled white) holds a glowing clock. A price tag swings from it. | `PRICE` tag |
| 2 | "…not the value of it." | Pull out. Behind the person is a huge outline shape (a building) that the person casts as a light beam. | `VALUE` pill |
| 3 | "Most people believe their paycheck…" | A paycheck slides in and lands on a balance scale opposite a "WORTH" weight. The scale balances. | `PAYCHECK = WORTH ?` |
| 4 | "But a salary is simply… so that you stay." | Glide to an office door. A chain-link draws from the paycheck to the person's ankle (soft, not dark). | `SO YOU STAY` |
| 5 | "Imagine earning twenty-five dollars an hour." | A clock face. Each tick drops a coin. | `$25 / HOUR` |
| 6 | "…forty hours a week… full year…" | A calendar grid fills row by row (52 rows flash by). | `40 × 52` |
| 7 | "…about fifty-two thousand dollars." | The counter rolls up with ease-out. Glow flash. | `$52,000` |
| 8 | "Now imagine your work helps the company bring in three times…" | Glide right. The company bar grows to 3× the person's bar. | `$156,000` |
| 9 | "Your value didn't change." | The person's light beam stays the same size. Push in. | `VALUE` |
| 10 | "Only the price of your time did." | Only the price tag swings and its number flickers. | `PRICE` |
| 11 | "…two people can work equally hard…" | Two identical people side by side, both with sweat-drop lines. | — |
| 12 | "One is paid for hours." | The left person: a clock above the head, coins trickle in one by one. | `HOURS` |
| 13 | "The other is paid for results." | The right person: a star or box above the head, coins pour in. | `RESULTS` |
| 14 | "And hours have a ceiling." | Camera tilts up. A hard horizontal line stops the left person's bar. | `CEILING` |
| 15 | "…two thousand working hours… never sell the same hour twice." | A row of hourglasses. One hourglass is grabbed and fades to grey. | `≈2,000 HOURS` |
| 16 | "Of course, a steady salary isn't a bad thing…" | A small house with a soft warm glow. A book appears beside it. | `PAYS THE BILLS` `TIME TO LEARN` |
| 17 | "…difference between your price and your value…" | The price tag and the value beam sit side by side. The gap between them is highlighted. | `THE GAP` |
| 18 | "Not how many hours can I sell…" | The clock icon dims to grey. | `HOURS?` |
| 19 | "…but what result can I create." | A box or star icon switches on with a bloom flash. | `RESULT` |
| 20 | "Your salary is the price of your time. Your value is what you build with it." | Pull out to the opening shot: the person with the clock, while the building behind glows brighter. This loops back to beat 1. | `BUILD` |
