The money that makes you happiest is often the money you give away. Most people think spending on themselves feels best. But in two thousand eight, psychologists Elizabeth Dunn, Lara Aknin and Michael Norton handed people an envelope with five or twenty dollars in the morning. Half were told to spend it on themselves. The other half had to spend it on someone else, or give it to charity. By evening, the people who spent on others were happier. And the amount, five or twenty, made no real difference. What mattered was who the money went to. Of course, you have to cover your own needs first. But this week, spend five dollars on someone else, and notice how it feels. Because the money that makes you happiest is often the money you give away.
