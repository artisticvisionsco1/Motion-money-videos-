# 01 — The first $10,000 (visual beats)

Style: black background, dot grid, white line-art, bloom. One continuous canvas with no cuts, ease-in-out glides. Each label is uppercase and tracked.

| # | Voice | Visual | Label / number |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "The first ten thousand dollars…" | The person (filled white) stands at the bottom of a steep hill. A glowing "$10,000" flag is at the top. | `$10,000` counts up |
| 2 | "…harder than the next hundred thousand." | Camera pulls out. Past the flag, a long gentle downhill slope leads to a bigger "$100,000" flag. | `$100,000` |
| 3 | "Most people believe… straight line" | A straight diagonal line draws on, with a glowing tip dot. Identical steps fall along it. | `STRAIGHT LINE` pill |
| 4 | "But money doesn't grow in a straight line." | The line bends into an exponential curve. Glow flash. | — |
| 5 | "Imagine saving three hundred dollars a month." | Glide to a jar. A coin drops in with a ripple ring. | `$300 / MONTH` |
| 6 | "…almost three years… ten thousand." | A calendar flips quickly. The jar fills slowly. The counter runs 0 → $9,900. | `33 MONTHS` |
| 7 | "…painfully slow." | The person sits beside the jar. A loading bar crawls along. | `SLOW` |
| 8 | "Invested at eight percent… eight hundred a year." | The jar becomes a small seed (morph). A tiny sprout appears. | `+$800 / YEAR` |
| 9 | "Barely noticeable." | Push in on the sprout: it is tiny. | — |
| 10 | "But at a hundred thousand… eight thousand a year" | Glide right. A big tree glows. Coins fall like leaves. | `+$8,000 / YEAR` |
| 11 | "…without you lifting a finger." | The person rests under the tree, arms behind the head. | — |
| 12 | "Your money starts doing part of the work." | A second small figure made of coins works beside the person. | `MONEY WORKS` pill |
| 13 | "…not only about interest." | Pull out. Three icons appear: `EARN` `SAVE` `SAY NO`. | 3 pills pop in staggered |
| 14 | "Those habits make every next step easier." | Stairs. Each step gets lower, and the person climbs faster. | — |
| 15 | "This is why most people quit early." | A crowd of outline people at the bottom of the hill. Most turn around and fade. | `QUIT EARLY` |
| 16 | "…judge the whole journey by its slowest part." | A magnifier sits only on the steep first segment of the full route. | `SLOWEST PART` |
| 17 | "Of course, investing always carries risk…" | The curve wobbles and dips once. | `NOT GUARANTEED` (grey) |
| 18 | "…isn't the middle. It's the beginning." | The camera rushes back along the route to the hill start. Spotlight beam. | `THE BEGINNING` |
| 19 | "…isn't just money. It's proof…" | The $10,000 flag becomes a glowing badge on the person's chest. | `PROOF` |
| 20 | "…makes the next hundred thousand possible." | Camera pulls out to the opening wide shot: hill → flag → slope → $100,000. This loops back to beat 1. | `$100,000` glows |
