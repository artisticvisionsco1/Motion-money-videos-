Your spending grows to fill whatever you earn. In nineteen fifty-five, the historian Cyril Northcote Parkinson wrote that work expands to fill the time available for its completion. Give a task a week, and it takes a week. Money works the same way. Most people think they'll save once they earn more. But a bigger paycheck quietly finds new places to go. A bigger food budget. A nicer phone plan. A few more subscriptions. Of course, some of that is fine. But if you want to save, don't leave the money lying around. Move it out the day you get paid, and live on what's left. Because your spending grows to fill whatever you earn, unless you give it less room.
