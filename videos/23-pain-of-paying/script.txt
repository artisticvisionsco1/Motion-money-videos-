Paying with a card makes money feel less real. Most people think a dollar is a dollar, however you pay. But in two thousand one, MIT professors Drazen Prelec and Duncan Simester auctioned real tickets to a sold-out Boston Celtics game. Half the bidders had to pay in cash. The other half paid by credit card. The card group bid about twice as much. Same tickets. Same game. Just a different way to pay. Cash hurts a little when it leaves your hand. A card removes that pain, so spending feels easy. Of course, cards are safe and useful. But for your fun spending, try one week with cash or a debit card, and see if the total drops. Because paying with a card makes money feel less real, and the bill is always real.
